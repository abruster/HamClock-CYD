Understanding ISS Passes

Brightness (Magnitude): Lower numbers = brighter. -2 to -4 are spectacular passes visible even in light-polluted areas.

Elevation: Maximum height above horizon. 90° = directly overhead. Passes above 40° are excellent viewing opportunities.

Direction: Where the ISS appears (Start) and disappears (End). Example: NW→SE means appears in northwest, travels to southeast.

Duration: How long the pass lasts. Longer passes give more viewing time.

Best Viewing: Look for passes with brightness better than 0.0 magnitude and elevation above 30°.
